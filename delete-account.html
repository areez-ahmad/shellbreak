Delete Your Security Academy Account

At Security Academy, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal information. If you wish to permanently delete your Security Academy account and associated data, please follow the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email from the email address associated with your Security Academy account to:

cellsec@areezahmad.me

Email Subject

Security Academy Account Deletion Request

Please Include

  • Your Full Name
  • Your Registered Email Address
  • Your Username (if available)
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted

  • Your account information
  • Your profile
  • Your learning progress
  • Your completed courses
  • Your quiz history
  • Your earned certificates
  • Your community posts and comments (or anonymized where technically required)
  • Your saved preferences

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable law, fraud prevention, security investigations, or to comply with legal obligations. Any retained information will be securely stored and deleted after the required retention period expires.

Processing Time

Most account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.
